Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have an eye exam?

Adults should have comprehensive eye exams every 1-2 years, or more frequently if you have diabetes, high blood pressure, a family history of eye disease, or are over 60.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, we offer telemedicine consultations for certain conditions and follow-up visits. Please call our office to determine if a virtual visit is appropriate for your needs.

Can I get my prescription filled at your office?

We provide prescriptions for eyeglasses and contact lenses. We can also help you order contacts directly through our office for your convenience.

What should I do if I lose or break my glasses?

Contact our office and we can provide you with a copy of your current prescription or schedule an exam if your prescription needs updating.
