Surgical Procedures

Our practice offers state-of-the-art surgical treatments performed with precision, care, and the latest technology. Dr. Bhargava has extensive experience in performing a wide range of ophthalmologic surgical procedures.

Cataract Surgery

Modern cataract surgery is one of the safest and most successful procedures in medicine. Using advanced phacoemulsification technique and premium intraocular lenses, we can restore clear vision and reduce dependence on glasses.

What to Expect:

  • Outpatient procedure typically taking 15-20 minutes
  • Local anesthesia with minimal discomfort
  • Small incision with quick healing
  • Removal of cloudy lens and insertion of artificial lens
  • Most patients notice improved vision within days
  • Options for multifocal or toric lenses to correct astigmatism

Benefits: Restored clarity, improved color perception, reduced glare, and enhanced quality of life with better vision for daily activities.

LASIK & Refractive Surgery

LASIK (Laser-Assisted In Situ Keratomileusis) is a popular vision correction procedure that can reduce or eliminate the need for glasses or contact lenses by reshaping the cornea to improve how light focuses on the retina.

What to Expect:

  • Comprehensive pre-operative evaluation to determine candidacy
  • Quick outpatient procedure lasting about 15 minutes
  • Advanced laser technology for precise corneal reshaping
  • Numbing eye drops for comfort during procedure
  • Rapid visual recovery, often within 24-48 hours
  • Follow-up appointments to monitor healing

Benefits: Freedom from glasses and contacts, improved vision for nearsightedness, farsightedness, and astigmatism.

Glaucoma Surgery

When medications and laser treatments are insufficient to control glaucoma, surgical intervention may be necessary to lower eye pressure and preserve vision. We offer several surgical options tailored to each patient's needs.

Procedures Include:

  • Trabeculectomy: Creating a new drainage channel to lower eye pressure
  • Tube Shunt Surgery: Implanting a drainage device to manage pressure
  • Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS): Less invasive options with faster recovery
  • Laser Trabeculoplasty: Laser treatment to improve drainage

Benefits: Reduced intraocular pressure, prevention of further optic nerve damage, and preservation of remaining vision.

Retinal Surgery

Retinal surgery is performed to treat serious conditions affecting the retina, including retinal detachment, macular holes, diabetic retinopathy complications, and epiretinal membranes.

Procedures Include:

  • Vitrectomy: Removal of vitreous gel to access and repair the retina
  • Retinal Detachment Repair: Reattaching the retina using laser, gas bubble, or silicone band
  • Macular Hole Repair: Surgical closure of holes in the macula
  • Membrane Peeling: Removal of scar tissue from the retinal surface

Benefits: Prevention of vision loss, improvement in vision quality, and treatment of potentially blinding conditions.

Corneal Transplant Surgery

Corneal transplantation (keratoplasty) replaces damaged or diseased corneal tissue with healthy donor tissue. This procedure can restore vision in patients with corneal scarring, keratoconus, or corneal dystrophies.

Types of Transplants:

  • Penetrating Keratoplasty (PK): Full-thickness corneal replacement
  • Endothelial Keratoplasty (DSEK/DMEK): Partial-thickness transplant of inner layers
  • Deep Anterior Lamellar Keratoplasty (DALK): Partial-thickness transplant of outer layers

Benefits: Restored corneal clarity, improved vision, relief from pain and discomfort, and treatment of progressive corneal conditions.

Pterygium Surgery

Pterygium is a growth of tissue on the white of the eye that can extend onto the cornea. Surgical removal is recommended when it affects vision, causes discomfort, or for cosmetic reasons.

What to Expect:

  • Outpatient procedure under local anesthesia
  • Removal of abnormal tissue growth
  • Graft placement to reduce recurrence risk
  • Recovery time of a few weeks
  • Follow-up care to monitor healing

Benefits: Improved appearance, relief from irritation, prevention of vision obstruction, and reduced recurrence with modern techniques.

Oculoplastic Surgery

Oculoplastic surgery addresses conditions affecting the eyelids, tear ducts, and orbital structures. These procedures can be both functional and cosmetic.

Procedures Include:

  • Ptosis Repair: Correction of drooping eyelids
  • Ectropion/Entropion Repair: Correction of eyelid position abnormalities
  • Chalazion Removal: Removal of eyelid cysts
  • Eyelid Lesion Excision: Removal of growths or tumors
  • Tear Duct Surgery: Treatment of blocked tear ducts

Benefits: Improved eyelid function, enhanced appearance, relief from irritation, and restoration of proper tear drainage.

YAG Laser Capsulotomy

After cataract surgery, some patients develop posterior capsule opacification (PCO), also called "secondary cataract." YAG laser capsulotomy is a quick, painless procedure to restore clear vision.

What to Expect:

  • In-office laser procedure taking just a few minutes
  • No incisions or anesthesia required
  • Creation of an opening in the cloudy capsule
  • Immediate improvement in vision
  • Return to normal activities right away

Benefits: Quick restoration of clear vision without the need for traditional surgery.
