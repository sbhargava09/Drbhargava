Surgical Procedures

Dr. Bhargava specializes in advanced cataract and glaucoma surgery, offering the latest surgical techniques and technology. With fellowship training and extensive experience, every procedure is performed with precision, expertise, and personalized care.

Cataract Surgery

Modern cataract surgery is one of the safest and most successful procedures in medicine. Using advanced phacoemulsification technique and premium intraocular lenses, we can restore clear vision and reduce dependence on glasses.

What to Expect:

  • Outpatient procedure typically taking 15-20 minutes
  • Local anesthesia with minimal discomfort
  • Small incision with quick healing
  • Removal of cloudy lens and insertion of artificial lens
  • Most patients notice improved vision within days
  • Options for multifocal or toric lenses to correct astigmatism

Benefits: Restored clarity, improved color perception, reduced glare, and enhanced quality of life with better vision for daily activities.

Trabeculectomy

Trabeculectomy is a traditional and highly effective glaucoma surgery that creates a new drainage pathway for fluid to leave the eye, thereby lowering intraocular pressure. This procedure is often recommended for advanced glaucoma or when other treatments have failed.

What to Expect:

  • Outpatient surgical procedure under local anesthesia
  • Creation of a small flap in the sclera (white of the eye)
  • Formation of a filtering bleb to drain excess fluid
  • Careful post-operative management to optimize healing
  • Long-term pressure reduction in most patients

Benefits: Significant and sustained reduction in intraocular pressure, proven track record of preventing vision loss from glaucoma.

Glaucoma Tube Shunt Implantation

Tube shunt surgery involves implanting a small drainage device to help regulate eye pressure in patients with glaucoma. This procedure is particularly beneficial for patients with complex or refractory glaucoma that hasn't responded to other treatments.

What to Expect:

  • Outpatient procedure performed under local or general anesthesia
  • Implantation of a small tube connected to a drainage plate
  • Tube redirects fluid to a reservoir under the conjunctiva
  • Effective for various types of glaucoma
  • Lower risk of scarring compared to trabeculectomy

Benefits: Reliable pressure control, excellent option for complex glaucoma cases, proven long-term success rates.

Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS)

MIGS procedures are newer surgical techniques that offer effective pressure reduction with faster recovery and fewer complications than traditional glaucoma surgery. These procedures can often be combined with cataract surgery.

What to Expect:

  • Micro-incisional approach with minimal tissue disruption
  • Often performed in conjunction with cataract surgery
  • Quick procedure with rapid visual recovery
  • Lower risk profile compared to traditional surgery
  • Effective for mild to moderate glaucoma

Benefits: Faster recovery, excellent safety profile, can reduce or eliminate need for glaucoma medications.

Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty (SLT)

SLT is an in-office laser procedure that lowers eye pressure by improving the drainage of fluid through the trabecular meshwork. This non-invasive treatment is often used as a first-line therapy or to reduce dependence on glaucoma medications.

What to Expect:

  • Quick in-office procedure taking only a few minutes
  • Numbing eye drops for comfort
  • Laser applied to drainage angle of the eye
  • No incisions or stitches required
  • Minimal discomfort during and after procedure
  • Can be repeated if necessary

Benefits: Non-invasive, effective pressure reduction, minimal side effects, can reduce or eliminate need for eye drops.

YAG Peripheral Iridotomy (YAG PI)

YAG PI is a laser procedure that creates a small opening in the iris to prevent or treat angle-closure glaucoma. This procedure allows fluid to flow more freely within the eye, reducing the risk of dangerous pressure spikes.

What to Expect:

  • Quick in-office laser procedure
  • Creation of a tiny hole in the peripheral iris
  • Prevents pupillary block and angle closure
  • No incisions or anesthesia required
  • Immediate effect in opening drainage angles
  • Return to normal activities right away

Benefits: Prevention of acute angle-closure attacks, protection against vision-threatening pressure spikes, simple and safe procedure.

YAG Laser Capsulotomy

After cataract surgery, some patients develop posterior capsule opacification (PCO), also called "secondary cataract." YAG laser capsulotomy is a quick, painless procedure to restore clear vision.

What to Expect:

  • In-office laser procedure taking just a few minutes
  • No incisions or anesthesia required
  • Creation of an opening in the cloudy capsule
  • Immediate improvement in vision
  • Return to normal activities right away

Benefits: Quick restoration of clear vision without the need for traditional surgery.
