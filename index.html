About Dr. Bhargava

Welcome! I am Dr. Bhargava, a fellowship-trained glaucoma and cataract surgeon dedicated to preserving and restoring vision through specialized surgical care. With advanced training and extensive experience in glaucoma management and cataract surgery, I am committed to providing the highest level of surgical expertise to my patients.

My practice focuses exclusively on glaucoma and cataract conditions, allowing me to offer specialized, expert care using the most advanced surgical techniques and technology. Whether you need cataract surgery to restore clear vision or glaucoma surgery to preserve your sight, my team and I are here to provide you with exceptional surgical care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Specialized Services

Cataract Surgery

Advanced cataract surgery using modern phacoemulsification techniques and premium intraocular lenses to restore clear vision. Same-day, outpatient procedures with excellent outcomes.

Glaucoma Management

Comprehensive medical and surgical management of glaucoma including tube shunt implantation, trabeculectomy, and minimally invasive glaucoma surgery (MIGS) to preserve vision.

Laser Procedures

In-office laser treatments including SLT (Selective Laser Trabeculoplasty) for glaucoma, YAG Peripheral Iridotomy for angle-closure prevention, and YAG Capsulotomy for post-cataract care.

Surgical Consultations

Expert surgical evaluations to determine the best treatment approach for your condition. Detailed discussions of surgical options, risks, benefits, and expected outcomes.

Post-Operative Care

Comprehensive follow-up care after cataract and glaucoma surgeries to ensure optimal healing and visual outcomes. Close monitoring and management of recovery.

Second Opinions

Expert second opinions for complex glaucoma and cataract cases. Evaluation of treatment recommendations and discussion of alternative surgical approaches.
