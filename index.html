About Dr. Bhargava

Welcome! I am Dr. Bhargava, a board-certified ophthalmologist dedicated to providing comprehensive eye care to patients of all ages. With years of experience in medical and surgical ophthalmology, I am committed to helping you achieve and maintain optimal vision throughout your life.

My practice combines state-of-the-art technology with personalized care to diagnose and treat a wide range of eye conditions. Whether you need a routine eye examination, treatment for an eye disease, or advanced surgical intervention, my team and I are here to provide you with the highest quality care in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Our Services

Comprehensive Eye Exams

Thorough eye examinations using the latest diagnostic equipment to assess your vision and eye health. Early detection is key to preventing vision loss.

Medical Eye Care

Expert diagnosis and treatment of various eye conditions including cataracts, glaucoma, macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and dry eye syndrome.

Surgical Excellence

Advanced surgical procedures including cataract surgery, LASIK, corneal transplants, and retinal surgeries performed with precision and care.

Pediatric Eye Care

Specialized care for children's eye health, including vision screening, amblyopia treatment, and management of pediatric eye conditions.

Emergency Eye Care

Prompt treatment for eye emergencies including infections, injuries, foreign bodies, and sudden vision changes. Same-day appointments available.

Contact Lens Services

Complete contact lens evaluations, fittings, and follow-up care for all types of contact lenses including specialty lenses.
